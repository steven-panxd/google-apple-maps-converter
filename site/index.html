  2. Paste it above.
  3. Tap Open in Apple Maps.

Works with places, dropped pins, searches and directions, including stops and travel mode.

Questions

How do I convert an Apple Maps link to Google Maps?

Paste the maps.apple.com link above. The converter detects it and gives you a Google Maps link that works on Android, Windows and in any browser.

Why do Google Maps links open in Safari instead of Apple Maps on iPhone?

iOS opens google.com/maps links in the Google Maps app or the browser. Converting the link to maps.apple.com makes it open in Apple Maps.

Is my location or link sent anywhere?

No. Links are converted in your browser and never leave your device.

Which links can't be converted?

Short links (maps.app.goo.gl) and links with only a place ID (?cid=). Open them in a browser and paste the full address instead, or use the iPhone shortcut, which handles short links.

Can I use this from an app or shortcut?

Yes. Open this page with ?url= and the link, plus &go=1 to jump straight to the map. It's also an open-source JavaScript library on GitHub.
