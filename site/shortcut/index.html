Open Google Maps links in Apple Maps on iPhone

A free shortcut puts Open in Apple Maps in your share sheet. Share any Google Maps link to it and Apple Maps opens at the same place, pin or route. Setup takes about ten seconds.

Get the shortcut

Step 1

Add the shortcut

On your iPhone, open the shortcut link. The Shortcuts app shows Open in Apple Maps. Tap Add Shortcut.

Step 2

Long-press a Google Maps link and tap Share

It works wherever the link is: Messages, WhatsApp, WeChat, Mail, Notes or a web page.

Try it now: long-press this link and tap Share.
google.com/maps/place/Eiffel+Tower

Step 3

Tap Open in Apple Maps

Scroll down the share sheet to find it, next to the green map pin.

Step 4, first time only

Tap Always Allow

iOS asks whether the shortcut may open the link, once for each website it passes through. Tap Always Allow each time and it won't ask again.

Step 5

Apple Maps opens at the same place

Places, dropped pins, searches and routes all carry over, including walking, transit and driving directions. Short maps.app.goo.gl links work too.

Tips

The link is on my clipboard, not in a message

Run the shortcut from the Shortcuts app or a Home Screen widget. With nothing shared to it, it reads the link from your clipboard.

Apple Maps opened a spot next to the place I wanted

That happens when you share the page while looking at it in Google Maps in Safari: Google rewrites the address and drops the exact pin. Share the original link instead, by long-pressing it in the message it came in.

Can I convert a link without the shortcut?

Yes. Paste it into the web converter. It also turns Apple Maps links into Google Maps links for friends on Android.
