Open Google Maps links in Apple Maps on iPhone

A free shortcut that adds Open in Apple Maps to your share sheet. It keeps the exact pin, the route and how you're travelling, and converts on your phone.

Get the shortcut

How it looks

Step 1

Add the shortcut

Open the shortcut on your iPhone and tap Add Shortcut.

Step 2

Share a Google Maps link

Open the link. It opens in Google Maps or Safari: tap Share, then Open in Apple Maps. In Safari you can also long-press a link and tap Share….

Try it:
google.com/maps/place/Eiffel+Tower

Step 3, first time only

Tap Always Allow

iOS asks once whether the shortcut may send the link to Maps. Short links (maps.app.goo.gl) ask once more, so Google can say where they point.

Step 4

Apple Maps opens

Same place, pin or route, travel mode included.

Tips

Messages or WeChat won't share the link

Copy the link, then run the shortcut from the Shortcuts app (or its Home Screen icon): it reads the clipboard.

Someone sent an address, not a link

Share the text the same way; the shortcut searches it in Apple Maps.

Apple Maps opened a spot nearby

Share the original link from the message, not the Google Maps page in Safari, which drops the exact pin.

Without the shortcut?

Paste the link into the web converter.
